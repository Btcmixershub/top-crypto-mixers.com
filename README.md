## Crypto Mixer Privacy Score Explained

Every service on the top list gets a privacy score. Here's what goes into it:

- **Pool depth.** How much liquidity the service holds for each coin. Deeper pools mean better mixing.
- **Timing protection.** Whether random or custom delays are available, and how long they can be.
- **Amount protection.** Multiple outputs, uneven distribution and a variable fee.
- **Data handling.** How long logs are kept and whether deletion is automatic.
- **Access privacy.** A working onion mirror and no JavaScript requirement.
- **Address hygiene.** Whether deposit addresses are ever reused.

A high score in one area doesn't make up for a weak one in another. The top crypto mixers do well across all six.

## How to Use a Crypto Mixer Safely

1. **Pick a service** from the current list on [top-crypto-mixers.com](https://top-crypto-mixers.com/).
2. **Open it in Tor Browser** using the verified onion link.
3. **Check the domain** character by character.
4. **Choose the coin** and enter two or more fresh receiving addresses.
5. **Set a random delay** and the payout distribution.
6. **Save the letter of guarantee** and verify its PGP signature.
7. **Send the deposit** in one transaction.
8. **Wait for the payouts** and keep the mixed coins in a separate wallet.

```bash
# verify the guarantee letter
gpg --import service_key.asc
gpg --verify letter_of_guarantee.txt
```

## Crypto Mixer Red Flags

- 🚩 The service only shows up in ads, not in independent top lists
- 🚩 Fees far below the market average
- 🚩 No letter of guarantee, or a signature that fails verification
- 🚩 A domain that's one letter off from the original
- 🚩 Requests for an email address, phone number or ID
- 🚩 "Limited-time bonus" or other pressure to send now
- 🚩 Supports a coin but has no real liquidity for it

## Top Crypto Mixers FAQ

### What are the top crypto mixers in 2026?
The list changes as services open, close and update their terms. The current top crypto mixers for each coin are on [top-crypto-mixers.com](https://top-crypto-mixers.com/).

### Which coins can I mix?
Most top mixers support Bitcoin. Many also support Ethereum, Litecoin and Bitcoin Cash, and some support tokens on EVM networks.

### What's the difference between a crypto mixer and a Bitcoin mixer?
A Bitcoin mixer works only with BTC. A crypto mixer supports several coins, each with its own pool and fees.

### How much do crypto mixers charge?
Usually about 1% to 5% plus network fees. Fees differ by coin and service.

### How long does mixing take?
From about 30 minutes to 24 hours or more, depending on the coin, network confirmations and the delay you set.

### Is it safe to use a crypto mixer?
It's much safer when you use a service from a maintained top list, connect over Tor and verify the letter of guarantee. Most losses come from clones, not from real mixers.

### Do top crypto mixers require registration?
No. A trustworthy mixer never asks for an account, email address or documents.

### Are crypto mixers legal?
Laws differ by country. Check the rules where you live before using any mixing service.
